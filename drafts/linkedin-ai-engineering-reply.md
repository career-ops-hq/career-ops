Depends on what you mean. These days "AI engineering" usually means building products on top of existing models: LLM pipelines, agents, RAG, that kind of thing. Training your own models is usually called ML engineering.

I haven't done either professionally. My background is smart contracts, mostly Solana and EVM. I do use agentic AI tooling heavily in my day-to-day work, so I know the ecosystem well from the builder-adjacent side.

The fake-account detector sounds like a fun problem though. My guess is the core of it is classic ML classification on profile features, network graphs and posting patterns, with an LLM layer on top for analyzing the actual content. Happy to jam on it if you start digging in.
