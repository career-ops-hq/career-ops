# Aztec - Reply to Rejection Email (Travis)

## Context

Received rejection from Travis after the pair programming interview. He kept the door open: "would like to keep your resume in mind for future opportunities and connect with you down the road."

## Goals for this reply

- Thank him for closing the loop (uncommon in crypto, where ghosting is the norm).
- Acknowledge the pair programming session honestly without grovelling.
- Respect Aztec's high senior bar; don't ask them to lower it.
- Propose growing into the senior role from a sub-senior level (with comp band to match).
- Tie the growth proposal to his "keep in touch" offer so it doesn't read as pushy.

## Draft

Hi Travis,

Thanks for closing the loop on this. In an industry where ghosting after final-round interviews has become the norm, I genuinely appreciate getting a clear answer.

On the pair programming session: I won't pretend it went the way I wanted, and I respect the bar Aztec is holding for its senior engineering hires. That bar is part of why I wanted to work with the team in the first place, and I'd rather see you keep it high than lower it.

That said, I want to put one thing on the table before we wrap up. Not meeting the senior bar today doesn't mean I'm not motivated to grow into it. Through my education and across my professional career I've consistently taken on responsibilities a level above where I was at the time, and that pattern has never discouraged me; it's pushed me to work harder and perform above my nominal level. If Aztec would be open to it, I'd welcome a conversation about joining at a level (and compensation band) below senior, with a path to grow into the senior role over time.

That's also my honest answer to your offer to keep in touch for future opportunities: yes, please do. If something at the right level opens up sooner rather than later, I'd be glad to pick the conversation back up. Otherwise, happy to reconnect down the road.

Thanks again for the time you and the team invested in the process.

Best,
Iaroslav

## Open considerations before sending

1. **Comp concession risk.** Volunteering a lower band right after rejection can signal low self-worth and anchor you below market if they ever come back. Softer alternative: ask whether they have non-senior openings where my profile fits, and leave compensation off the table until they engage.
2. **Pair programming acknowledgment.** "I won't pretend it went the way I wanted" is honest and mature, but could be dropped entirely. Leaving it in shows self-awareness; cutting it keeps the message forward-looking.
