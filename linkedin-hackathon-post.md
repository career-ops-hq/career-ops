## Posting strategy

LinkedIn doesn't allow attaching photos to a reshare/quote-repost, and quote-reposts get throttled algorithmically anyway. So:

1. Post original content with the 2 photos.
2. Tag **Frontiers Iași** and **Paul Berg** in the body using LinkedIn's @-mention (so they get notified and the algorithm cross-pollinates to their networks).
3. Put the link to the event's original post **in the first comment, not the body**.

### Why the link goes in the comment

LinkedIn deprioritizes posts that contain links (even links to LinkedIn itself) because they push users off the feed. The first-comment-link trick is the standard workaround and reliably outperforms posts with the same link in the body. Add a line in the body like "link to the event in the comments" so people know where to look.

## Draft

🏆 Happy to share that @Paul Berg and I won 1st place at @Frontiers Iași's inaugural 4-hour hackathon.

We built **Investescu**, an AI-powered local tax guide for Romanian retail investors that answers two questions:

1. Is this stock available through a Romanian broker?
2. Where to buy, where to hold, and where/when to sell to keep taxes and broker fees as low as possible?

💡 We saw a clear gap: Romanian retail investors quietly pay more tax and broker fees than they need to, because local and foreign brokers are taxed differently and custody fees stack on top. Investescu closes that gap - it's a Telegram bot wired to an AI backend with skills purpose-built for these decisions.

🛠️ Solid 4 hours. Would do it again.
