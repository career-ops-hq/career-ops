# Mode: interview-redflag — Evidence-tiered process review

Resolve `interview-context.mjs` first. Analyse only sessions whose canonical
company and role match that opportunity.

This review applies only to Mainland China, Hong Kong, or a role explicitly
remote with an applicable jurisdiction supplied by the candidate. If none
applies, report that jurisdiction is unknown and do not infer legal or
employment-practice rules from another market.

For each observation, retain the verbatim evidence and assign a tier:

| Tier | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| observation | one interview exchange | report without verdict |
| corroborated | same concern across two sessions or an official source | ask a specific follow-up |
| material pattern | corroborated concern affecting scope, process, or safety | recommend reconsideration |

Produce a company-level note under `interview-prep/` with the sessions and
sources used. Never write `data/blacklist.md`, never label a specific exchange
illegal, and never treat an external review as proof. The candidate decides
whether to proceed.
