# Blueprint: AI training & evaluation expert (gig platforms, EN)

All facts come from `cv.md`, `article-digest.md` and `config/profile.yml` at run time (see AGENTS.md, Source-of-Truth Boundary). Placeholders in `{braces}` are filled only from those files; leave an item out rather than inventing it. Output language follows `language.output` in `config/profile.yml` unless the blueprint name says otherwise.

Use with mode `gig`. Best for AI data-work platforms (DataAnnotation, Outlier, micro1, Mercor, Alignerr, Handshake AI, Surge, Mindrift): expert-tier onboarding and domain-expert gigs. Target ~450 words, one page.

## Structure

# {candidate_name} — AI Training & Evaluation Expert
Headline: `{evaluation_tenure} · {native_language} native · {degrees} · {domain_background}`
Contact line. Availability line from profile facts, else `{availability}`.

## Expertise areas
One bullet per area, strongest first: `Area — proof`. Only areas evidenced in the sources (e.g. native language, code review, mathematics/logic, a professional domain, security/GRC).

## AI evaluation track record
`### {evaluation_role_title} — {platforms} ({dates})` with 4–5 bullets in platform vocabulary (response rating/ranking, rubric grading, side-by-side comparison, factuality and safety review, red-teaming, code/math verification, defect documentation) — only where true.

## Relevant work
3–4 compressed entries (one line + at most 2 bullets) with a domain-expertise angle.

## Education
Degrees and thesis titles from the sources; earlier degrees in one line.

## Languages
`{language} ({level})` per evidenced language.

## Tools & setup
Evaluation tooling named in the sources only, plus timezone and remote setup.
