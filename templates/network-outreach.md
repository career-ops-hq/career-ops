# Network Outreach Templates
# Quiet search — no "Open to Work" signals
# Use these for reaching out to ex-colleagues and warm connections

## Template 1: Ex-Amazon / Ex-Shopify — Casual Check-in

> Hey [Name],
>
> Hope you're doing well. Been a while since [Amazon Robotics / 6 River / Shopify].
>
> I'm still at Butlr building the technical operations and professional services org — grew it from $0 to ~$7M in 18 months, now 55% of revenue. Pretty wild ride.
>
> I'm starting to look at what's next and thought of you. Do you know of any ops leadership roles (VP / Sr. Director level) at companies doing hardware/IoT deployment at scale? Or anyone I should talk to?
>
> Not in a rush — just planting seeds. Would love to catch up either way.
>
> Brian

**Best for:** People you know well but haven't spoken to in 6-12 months.
**Tone:** Casual, not desperate, emphasizes current success.
**When to send:** Any time. No urgency signal.

## Template 2: Direct Ask for Intro to Hiring Manager

> Hey [Name],
>
> Quick ask: I'm exploring VP / Sr. Director ops roles at established companies (public or late-stage) doing hardware/IoT deployment. Remote-first is my preference.
>
> Do you know anyone at [Company Name] in ops leadership? I'd love a warm intro if the relationship allows — happy to send you a one-paragraph blurb you can forward.
>
> No pressure if not. Thanks either way.
>
> Brian

**Best for:** People who are well-connected and you have a solid relationship with.
**Tone:** Direct but low-pressure. Specific ask, easy out.
**When to send:** When you identify a specific company/role through the scanner.

## Template 3: Avik / Client Network — Leveraging Shared History

> Hey [Name],
>
> Long time no talk — those Amazon Go installs feel like a different lifetime now.
>
> I'm Head of Technical Operations at Butlr (IoT/smart buildings). Built the whole ops and PS function from zero — $7M revenue in 18 months, global vendor network across 20+ countries, the whole thing.
>
> Starting to think about what's next. Do you know of any ops leadership roles at companies deploying hardware at scale? Or anyone in your network I should meet?
>
> Always good to hear from you.
>
> Brian

**Best for:** Avik colleagues, former clients, vendors you worked with closely.
**Tone:** Nostalgic but forward-looking. Shared history creates instant rapport.
**When to send:** Any time. These people know your work ethic firsthand.

## Template 4: Automated Tire / Consultancy — Positioning as Advisor

> Hey [Name],
>
> Quick update: in addition to my role at Butlr, I've started advising hardware/IoT companies on ops strategy — deployment playbooks, vendor networks, PS org design, CMMS selection, that sort of thing.
>
> Currently working with a Boston-based company on their technical support infrastructure. It's been a great way to stay sharp and help companies that need VP-level ops expertise without the full-time overhead.
>
> If you know any founders or ops leaders who could use that kind of help, send them my way. And if you hear of any full-time VP/Sr. Director ops roles at solid companies, I'd love a heads up.
>
> Brian

**Best for:** People in startup/VC ecosystems, advisors, board members, investors.
**Tone:** Positioning yourself as an advisor first, job seeker second.
**When to send:** After you have at least one consultancy client (Month 2+).

---

## Follow-Up Strategy

**Rule:** One follow-up max, 7-10 days later, if no response.

> Hey [Name] — quick bump on this. No rush, just wanted to make sure it didn't get buried.
>
> Brian

**Never:** Follow up more than once. If someone doesn't respond, move on. They'll come back if they have something.

## Tracking

Use a simple spreadsheet or your email sent folder to track:
- Who you reached out to
- Date sent
- Template used
- Response?
- Intro generated?
- Role discovered?

Or just check the `data/applications.md` tracker and add a "Network" column if you want to centralize everything.
